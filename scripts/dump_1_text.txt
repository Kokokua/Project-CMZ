--- Shape 'TextBox 8' (id=8) left=1.75, top=3.05, w=7.31, h=4.27 ---
  P0 [None, Nonept, bold=None]: 1. Downsampling to 2 FPS
  P1 [None, Nonept, bold=None]: • Sample 1 frame every 0.5 seconds (down from 15 FPS).
  P2 [None, Nonept, bold=None]: • 7.5x reduction in compute load (from 900 to 120 frames/min).
  P3 [None, Nonept, bold=None]: • Highly sufficient for animal behavior: a 10-second walk provides 20 distinct centroid coordinates.
  P4 [None, Nonept, bold=None]: • Filters out high-frequency camera noise while maintaining robust trajectory tracking.

--- Shape 'TextBox 11' (id=11) left=10.76, top=3.05, w=7.46, h=5.13 ---
  P0 [None, Nonept, bold=None]: 2. The 1-Minute State Rule
  P1 [None, Nonept, bold=None]: • Grounded in Zoo Protocol: 'Behavior changes if they spend more than 1 minute.'
  P2 [None, Nonept, bold=None]: • Active ➔ Inactive: Requires continuous stationary state for ≥ 1 full minute (120 frames) before transitioning to Resting.
  P3 [None, Nonept, bold=None]: • Inactive ➔ Active: Requires sustained activity for ≥ 1 full minute before switching to Active (prevents false wakeups from minor shifts).
  P4 [None, Nonept, bold=None]: • Short Walking Bursts (8–28s): Correctly logged as micro-events inside the Active window without being discarded.
